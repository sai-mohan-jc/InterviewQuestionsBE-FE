# Sample Banking KYC Policy

## 1. Purpose of KYC

Know Your Customer (KYC) is a process used by banks to verify the identity of their customers. Its purpose is to prevent identity fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing and other financial crimes.

## 2. Customer Identity Verification

The bank must collect and verify valid customer identity information before providing services that require KYC verification.

Examples of identity information include:
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Government-issued identity document
- Customer identification number

## 3. Address Verification

The bank must collect valid address information and verify it using acceptable supporting documents according to its internal procedures.

## 4. KYC Status

A customer can have one of the following KYC statuses:

- PENDING: Customer information has been submitted but verification is not yet completed.
- VERIFIED: Customer identity and required information have been successfully verified.
- REJECTED: Customer verification has failed or submitted information is insufficient.

## 5. Periodic KYC Updates

Customer information should be reviewed periodically according to the bank's internal risk classification and applicable regulatory requirements.

Customers may be asked to submit updated identity or address documents when required.

## 6. Failed KYC Verification

If KYC verification fails, the bank should investigate the reason and request corrected information or supporting documents where appropriate.

Certain banking services may be restricted until the required verification is successfully completed.

Note: This is a fictional sample policy created for software development and RAG learning. It is not an actual bank policy or regulatory document.
